Peer Comparison Experiment

Graduate research project, team of 3. Randomized online experiment in Qualtrics.

Research and healthCompleted

01Problem

Dashboards often show people how peers performed. Does that actually make them more accurate?

02Approach

Randomly split 40 participants into control and treatment, ran 11 counting tasks, measured effort and difficulty, and added a manipulation check.

03Impact

Treatment was 4.1 points more accurate (86.8% vs 82.7%), not significant at this size (p = 0.35). Only 44% of the treatment group noticed the peer information, so we specified a stronger design and a 200+ person study.

Results

What I did

  • Effort was slightly lower in the treatment group (2.50 vs 2.81 on a 4-point scale, p = 0.21).
