Book Recommender System

Web Mining and Recommender Systems coursework.

AI and NLPCompleted

01Problem

Predict whether a reader will pick up a book, how they will rate it and what genre a review describes.

02Approach

Matrix factorization with user and book biases, TF-IDF text features with a linear SVM, and item-to-item similarity.

03Impact

Three working prediction models. In a related classifier, review scores and length cut the error rate from 16.1% to 14.3%, and removing beer style more than doubled it to 31.4%, showing style carries most of the signal.

Results

What I did

  • Matrix factorization with user and book bias terms for rating prediction.
  • TF-IDF word and character n-grams with a calibrated linear SVM for genre.
  • Item-to-item recommender using Jaccard similarity on Amazon musical instrument reviews.
