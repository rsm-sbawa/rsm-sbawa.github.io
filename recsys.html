Book Recommender System

Web Mining and Recommender Systems coursework.

AI and NLPCompleted

01Problem

Predict whether a reader will pick up a book, how they will rate it and what genre a review describes.

02Approach

Matrix factorization with user and book biases, TF-IDF text features with a linear SVM, and item-to-item similarity.

03Impact

Three working prediction models; on a related task, added features cut a classifier's error from 16.1% to 14.3%.

Results

3models: would-read, rating and genre
