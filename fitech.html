FiTech: Testing Credit Card Offers

Customer Analytics, team project (Group 30). Two-round market simulation with 750,000 prospects.

Marketing analyticsCompleted

01Problem

FiTech had 12 card offers and 3 credit-risk segments. Past campaigns couldn't say which offer wins because they weren't randomized and the market had changed.

02Approach

Tested all 36 offer and segment cells with 4,000 prospects each, scored each cell on expected profit per email (response rate x lifetime value minus $0.50), then rolled the winner out per segment.

03Impact

$877K total profit. The rollout earned $846K against an $835K forecast and brought in about 23,800 cardholders worth $1.27M in lifetime value.

Results

What I did

  • Customers prefer the low-APR, no-fee, fixed-rate card, but it isn't the most profitable everywhere; higher-risk segments paid off with a higher APR.
  • Winners: offer 4 for low and medium risk, offer 12 for high risk.
