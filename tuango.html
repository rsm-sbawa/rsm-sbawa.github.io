Tuango: Who Should Get the Deal?

Customer Analytics. Individual project for a Chinese deals app.

Marketing analyticsCompleted

01Problem

Every push message costs money. Tuango needed to know which of 397,252 users to send a karaoke deal so the campaign makes a profit.

02Approach

Logistic regression for who buys, linear regression for how much, a breakeven response rate of about 9 to 10%, and then a check against the real rollout data instead of the model's own projections.

03Impact

On actual rollout results, targeting earned 887K RMB vs 112K RMB for messaging everyone, and return on marketing spend rose from 3.1% to 56.6%.

Results

What I did

  • 174,126 users scored above breakeven.
  • Permutation importance and prediction plots explained which customer traits drive purchase.
