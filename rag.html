Help Desk Assistant with RAG (Live Demo)

My original version was an MSBA elective team project (Claude + LlamaIndex, 88% on a 40-question test). Those files were lost, so I rebuilt it on synthetic data as a demo that runs in your browser.

AI and NLPCompleted

01Problem

A support bot that sounds confident but cites nothing is a liability. It should answer from the help center, show its source, use tools for live data, and decline when it doesn't know.

02Approach

Built a 23-article help center and 5 sample orders for a fictional craft store. Questions are routed to a tool (order lookup, shipping quote) or to BM25 retrieval, which picks the best passage and the most relevant sentences and cites the source. Low-scoring questions are declined.

03Impact

Tuned on a 40-question set (36 correct), then scored on 30 new questions: 24 correct with the right source (80%). Its weak spot is clear: it answered all 3 out-of-scope questions instead of declining, so a stricter refusal rule is the next fix.
