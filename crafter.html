Inventory Optimization for Crafter.com

MSBA capstone for an online craft retailer. I led a team of 4.

Forecasting and riskClient project2026

01Problem

Crafter's system logged out-of-stock months as zero sales. Every forecast built on that history learned to expect too little, so the best sellers kept running out.

02Approach

Integrated 6 data sources (176 products, 564K+ order lines), reconstructed 360 stockout months, then compared XGBoost, SARIMA, Ridge and a naive baseline on held-out months.

03Impact

Recovered about 6% of hidden demand for A-class products. XGBoost won (out-of-sample R² 0.48, MAPE about 12%), feeding a budget-constrained buy list with a projected 3.8x return.

Results

3.8xprojected return on a $15K inventory budget

What I did

  • Built the demand reconstruction so stockout months were filled with estimated demand instead of zeros.
  • Ran the model comparison on months the models never saw, so the winner was chosen on honest accuracy.
  • Turned the forecast into a buy list under a $15K budget, plus an interactive dashboard and an Excel planning toolkit the client's team can run without us.
