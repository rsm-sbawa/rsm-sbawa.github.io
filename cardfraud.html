Card Transaction Fraud Detection

Fraud Analytics. Individual project on 98,161 card transactions.

Forecasting and riskCompleted

01Problem

Card fraud has to be caught from spending patterns, and a model that only looks good on training data fails on next month's transactions.

02Approach

Built 2,644 candidate velocity and spending variables, selected 20, and tuned 25 configurations across 5 model families, judging each on a later time period.

03Impact

Random forest caught 57.8% of fraud in the riskiest 3% and 66.5% in the riskiest 5%, about $165K of fraud on the holdout (78.6% of its fraud dollars).

Results

What I did

  • Showed overfitting directly: deeper trees kept improving on training data while results on later months fell.
  • Picked random forest because it held up best on later data, even though XGBoost scored slightly higher on the test split.
