Procure-to-Pay ETL Pipeline in Snowflake

SQL and data warehousing coursework, team of 3.

BI and data engineeringCompleted

01Problem

Purchase orders, invoices and supplier files arrive as raw files. Finance teams need clean tables they can query safely.

02Approach

Staged raw files, loaded them with PUT and COPY INTO, cleaned types with TRY_TO_NUMBER and TRY_TO_DATE, matched supplier invoices to purchase order totals, and served the result through a materialized view. Then joined supplier ZIP codes to daily weather data.

03Impact

Finance can see, invoice by invoice, how far the amount billed differs from what the purchase order quoted, with weather on the transaction date alongside for analysis.

Results

StageLoadModelEnrichServe

What I did

  • Typed the raw CSV columns safely so bad values became nulls instead of failing the load.
  • Computed each purchase order's quoted amount (units received x expected unit price) and matched invoices on both order ID and supplier ID.
  • Extracted clean 5-digit ZIP codes from messy address fields with regular expressions before the weather join.
  • Granted warehouse and database access through roles instead of individual users.
  • One later query in our submission flipped the sign of invoiced vs quoted. The definition used here (invoiced minus quoted) is the one in the materialized view.
