Procure-to-Pay ETL Pipeline in Snowflake

SQL and data warehousing coursework, team of 3.

BI and data engineeringCompleted

01Problem

Purchase orders, invoices and supplier files arrive as raw files. Finance teams need clean tables they can query safely.

02Approach

Staged files, loaded them with PUT and COPY INTO, modeled procure-to-pay tables, joined external weather data and served results through a materialized view with role-based access.

03Impact

An end-to-end, repeatable pipeline from raw files to a governed analytics view.
