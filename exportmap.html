ExportMap: Export Market Finder

Team project, team of 4.

Research and healthCompleted

01Problem

Small exporters pick foreign markets by gut feel because trade, tariff and demographic data are scattered.

02Approach

Merged 7 datasets, standardized country codes, built a 0 to 1 Market Opportunity Score and modeled GDP per capita.

03Impact

A ranked shortlist of markets for a test product (children's clothing). The GDP model improved from R² 0.775 to 0.796.

Results

7public datasets merged into one country view
