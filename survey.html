Sydney Rental Housing Survey (Rebuilt)

Undergraduate sociology research project, University of Sydney. The original files were lost, so this is the questionnaire rebuilt as a working form.

Research and healthCompleted

01Problem

Rents in Sydney were rising fast. The project needed first-hand data on who was under pressure and how they were coping, asked in a way that doesn't lead the respondent.

02Approach

Neutral wording, ranges and single choices where precision isn't needed, an optional income question to reduce drop-off, a 5-point agreement scale for stress and a select-all list of coping actions.

03Impact

Each answer becomes a clean, analysis-ready record, including a rent-to-income ratio and the common 30% rental-stress flag. No results are shown here because the original responses were lost.
